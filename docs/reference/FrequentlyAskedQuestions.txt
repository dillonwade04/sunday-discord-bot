CSSO Frequently Asked Questions


Who?
* Who do I contact about a LOA?
   * Sergeant and above (Following COC)
* Who do I contact about a Strike Request?
   * Only Command Staff can request a Strike in the Leadership Discord on the designated channel.  Corporals - Master Sgts should follow COC to properly discuss a needed Strike for someone.
* Who can I give Ride-Alongs to?
   * Senior Deputy+ are Ride Along Certified. They may give anyone below their rank a ride along.
   * Deputy II + can give ride alongs ONLY to Cadets until you reach Senior Deputy.
   * Senior Deputies can give ride alongs to all the ranks below them until you reach Corporal.
* Who do I talk to about Transferring, Resigning or going Reserves?
   * Following COC, Any of our command staff can take a transfer request to be given to heads for approval.
   * Resignations can be given to a Sergeant.
   * Anyone requesting to be placed in reserves may speak to a Lieutenant +.
* Who can help me with Promoting someone?
   * If you are a Corporal or Sr. Corporal, following COC contact a Sergeant + to discuss your recommendation.
What?
* What does CSSO stand for?
   * Carolina State Sheriffs Office.
* What is Chain of Command?
   * Chain of Command refers to a chain of communication within the department. When you have a question about the department or need help you are to follow the chain of command and go to the rank directly above yours with the exception of LOA’s, Reserves, and Transfers.
* What is Leadership?
   * Leadership consists of any member that is Corporal or above.
Primary Leadership is Corporal and Senior Corporals.
* What are Supervisors?
   * Supervisors are all ranks Sergeant and above.
* What is Command Staff?
   * Command staff refers to the ranks of Lieutenant, Captains, Majors and all Heads.
* What are Heads?
   * Heads are the ones who are in charge of everything in the entire department. There are 7 Heads. The Sheriff, Chief Deputy, Undersheriff, Deputy Undersheriff, Commander, Deputy Commander, Admiral,
What is the Roster?
   * The Roster is a document where all Department Personnel are listed by rank, it displays Deputy Names, Abilities, Call signs, Promotion Dates, and other important statistics.




   * What are Sub-Divisions? How do I apply?
   * (SVU) Special Vehicles Unit, (SCIU) Special Criminal Investigations Unit, (SWAT) Special Weapons And Tactics,Gang & Narcotics Task Force(GNTF) , Traffic and K9. On the  CSSO discord,  you can apply to any subdivision in the #subdivision-applications channel.
   * What are the minimum required Patrol Hours?
   * 3 Hours minimum per week. (Unless you have been approved for lowered patrol hours or at the rank of deputy I)
   * What is an LOA?
   * An LOA is a leave of absence for up to 21 days. This is used for anyone who may be going on a trip or having issues IRL that will not allow you to be online and meet your weekly requirements.
   * What are Reserves?
   * Taking a Reserve is for any LOA that may extend from 21 days or more.
   * What is a Blacklist?
   * A Blacklist is a punishment issued for anyone who breaks rules to the extreme or most commonly is treasonous.  A blacklist means you may never apply to the CSSO again or join the department. Blacklists are issued by command staff and above. Each blacklist is approved by a head.
   * What is Treason?
   * Treason is the act of harming the CSRP Community, Image, Likeness or Physical Properties (documents) in a malicious way.
   * What is Dual-Clanning?
   * Dual-Clanning is when you play on any FiveM server that is not CSRP.  Anyone Leadership+ who plays on a FiveM server that is not CSRP will receive a blacklist.
   * What is a Strike?
   * A strike is a punishment given by the Chief Deputy according to our demerit system.  Any member of the CSSO that is breaking rules may receive strikes and other punishments. (Refer to CSSO Demerits in #csso-Information)
   * What is Top Cop?
   * Top Cop is a weekly competition we have for the most logged hours for CSSO.  The top 5 members of CSSO with the most logged hours in clockify each week will receive special vehicles to drive on duty for 7 days.
   * What is Casual Thursday?
   * Casual Thursday is our reward day for the department; meaning you can drive any marked CSSO vehicle except from the Demon, while still following  all our guidelines and policies. You can also make your own uniform for the day, it must have a sheriff marking and must be realistic / reasonable.
   * What are my Duties/Expectations for my current rank?
   * All duties and expectations of any given rank can be found in the CSSO Main Links found in #csso-Information. There is a small explanation of what is expected out of every rank in the CSSO.
   * What is RTO?
   * Radio Traffic Only. RTO is the LEO radio that we use to communicate with all LEO’s on duty. Should be used as realistically as possible.
   * What is Clockify?
   * Clockify is a time clock. Once registered you will use Clockify to track all your Patrol Hours and other department related time.
   * What Callsigns may I display in my nickname?
   * Only CSSO, Fire, FAA,DOW and DOT. Subdivision Callsigns are NOT allowed for any reason.
   * CSSO is to always be the first call sign in your name unless actively on duty with another.

When?




   * When can I get a Ride-Along?
   * As a Cadet you may get a ride along at any time from a Deputy II + by dropping a message in general or ride-along-request in discord. As a Probationary deputy and above you can request a ride along from a Sergeant at any time.

   * When is the next Advanced Training?
   * Advanced Trainings are not guaranteed but are normally once a week. They will always be announced in the discord at least 24 hours in advance.




   * When will I get Promoted?
   * Promotions are given to those who are deserving of one and have put in the time.
A few small words of advice I can give to expedite your promotion is to be active and patrol at least 5 hours a week. Alongside this we are looking for members actively engaging in chats and helping other members of the department out.  Up until the rank of Senior Deputy you can typically expect to be at a rank for at least 7 days before a promotion. Corporal and above your time at each rank will continue to be longer.  Also for Corporals and above you will be required to complete all your assigned duties before you will be considered for a promotion. Do your best and enjoy each rank, the promotions will follow..
   * When can I Patrol Alone?
   * Once you pass your Training Test and In Game Assessment, as a Deputy I you can Patrol Solo. Cadets are never permitted to patrol alone.
   * When do I need to call for a Supervisor on duty?
   * Always call for a Supervisor If an active scene involved the use of a firearm, injury or death or if a suspect requests a supervisor.
   * When can I use Body Armor/wear a Vest?
   * Deputy II + should always wear a Vest. Everyone may use a vest in a high priority situation such as a Bank Robbery. Once a priority is concluded you must take the vest off.
   * When do I need to read a suspect their Miranda Rights?
   * It doesn't matter whether an interrogation occurs in a jail, at the scene of a crime, on a busy downtown street, or the middle of an open field: If a person is in custody (deprived of his or her freedom of action in any significant way), the police must read the Miranda rights if they want to ask questions and use the answers as evidence at trial.

Can?
   * Can I Patrol with a Partner? (Non 10-12)
   * Yes. Deputy 1 and above.
   * Can I play on Drift or Flight Simulator Type Servers?
   * No, you cannot play on any other FiveM servers that are not CSRP related.
   * Can I get Promoted from a Ride-along?
   * Yes. There is absolutely no guarantee of promotion but it does happen. Don't ever expect a promotion from a ride along and as always don't ask about or for a promotion.
   * Can I put a Flag behind my badge?
   * Sergeant + may put the flag of their home country behind their badge.
   * Can I Log Hours for Trainings and Meetings?
   * Yes. Anything department related may be logged as hours in clockify.
   * Can I log my Sub-Division Hours as CSSO Hours?
   * Yes. Select the proper project in Clockify for which subdivision you are patrolling in.
Do?
   * Do I have to have a certain Licence Plate while on duty?
   * Yes. All Marked CSSO Patrol Vehicles Must display the White “SA EXEMPT” Plate and it Must show your Call Sign. Corporals + May also display their rank on their plate with their call sign. Check the Licence Plate Guide in the #csso-information channel for more details.


   * Do I have to attend Advanced Trainings?
   * No, But if you're online and available and the Advanced Training becomes “Mandatory” you must attend. We always recommend you attend Advanced Trainings, they are very informative, a lot of fun, and there are many different types of Advanced Trainings.




   * Do I have to have a Badge as my discord picture?
   * Once you are a Deputy I you MUST create a Badge and set it as your Discord Picture. No, there aren’t any Badge Exceptions unless High Staff approve them.
