Carolina State Sheriff’s Office
Strikes / Demerits Manual


Approved: J. Kingston


The strike system was put in place to keep deputies in line and active and acts as a way of punishment without an instant termination or other harsh disciplinary measures that could be enforced by the strike system.


The only ranks able to apply, and remove strikes are the Heads with the 03 taking the lead on most strikes. Strikes may be audited, and directly requested to the Heads (in most cases, the 04) by all Command Staff members, while any lower-ranking members (First Sergeant and below) must bring their strike requests to Command staff to then have them accepted and then requested to the heads.


Strikes last a month from the time they are given to a member of CSSO, and end a month after the strikes have been applied. A maximum of 4 strikes may be added to a member with any additional strikes resulting in a termination, and/or blacklist from the department.


Listed below are the offenses and point values for each strike. If you have any questions, contact your immediate supervisor through the Chain of Command.

Variable Point Offenses:
Variable point offenses are strikes that may have points factors changed depending on the case-to-case needs
1. Failure to Follow SOP
2. Verbal or Text disrespect
3. Breaking server rules such as: rdm, vdm, meta gaming
4. Creating tension with other departments
5. Creating, participating in, and/or welcoming drama between departments or department members.
6. Breaking Chain of Command
7. Verbal or Text disrespect


Possible Sanctions:
Sanctions are punishments that come with strikes. These sanctions last for the period of the strike(s) and may be added to, or substituted as a strike.
1. VA Order: Revoke the ability for a deputy to drive in any LEO vehicle while on duty unless in an emergency situation. This is essentially a foot patrol order, the use of any vehicle is prohibited while this order is in place.
2. VB Order: Revokes ability for the deputy to patrol in any vehicle alone and are bound to do ride alongs/utilize the buddy system to for use on duty.
3. DIT Order: DIT (Deputy in Training) obligates the deputy to complete a specific training course before they may resume active duty. This training must be signed off by a high command member to show valid completion.
4. Uniform Order: The deputy must use the lowest ranking in their main and sub division uniforms during all patrols for the set period of the strike(s).
5. Subdivision Order: Revokes the ability for a deputy to patrol in a subdivision for the period of their strikes. This requires the deputy to be placed on suspension in that subdivision for the time specified.
6. Supervisor Order: Requires the deputy to be over-watched by a supervisor at all times during the course of their duties. This requires the said deputy to ride along with a supervisor+. This applies to all aspects of CSSO, and you will need to be supervised on ALL aspects of your duties, ect patrolling, leadership duties, specialty duties, and subdivisions.
7. Probation: An extra close eye being on a deputy, any mess-ups is 2x the original sanction/strike. Signed off by a high command member or department head.
8. Promotion Hold: If you are not performing well at your rank you will be placed on a promotion hold. During this hold if you do not prove that you are able to do your duties, you will be demoted from your rank. The set time for this will be case by case.
9. Suspension: The said deputy placed on Suspension will be unable to do anything CSSO related during the duration of their suspension. This can be extended as needed.
