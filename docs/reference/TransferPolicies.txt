Carolina State Sheriff's Office Transfer Policies and Regulations


• Transfers are only given to CSSO Personnel who are on active duty.



• Once a transfer is accepted, the Deputy must wait a total of 2 weeks (14 days) before transfer is finalized

• During the transfer process the Deputy must remain in good standing for the duration of the transfer period. • If the Deputy is given any punishments during the duration of their transfer, the Deputy's transfer may be placed on hold and subject to a probationary period.


• A Deputy is expected to continue to meet their expectations for the duration of their transfer process, failure to do so may result in punishment.


• A Deputy may not transfer if they have any active strike or probations, these punishments must expire before transfer is approved.


• A Deputy cannot request a transfer within 2 weeks of any type of punishment. (Verbal Warnings. Probation, Strike, Demotion, etc.) Going LOA during the transfer process will place your transfer on hold until the LOA has concluded.


• If transferring after a span of 1 week at your rank you have the opportunity to be placed at a rank x1 lower than your current rank. For example, if you were a Corporal promoted to Senior Corporal and would like to transfer before the 7-day mark you will be placed at Corporal.


• If you are LOA or Reserves, you must wait 2 weeks from that point of coming back to an active position before you will be eligible to transfer.
